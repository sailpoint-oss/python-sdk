# CertificationReviewProgress

The progress of certification review. * `NOT_STARTED`: No certification item is reviewed yet. * `IN_PROGRESS`: Partial review is done on certification. * `DECISIONS_COMPLETE`: All the decision completed on certification, just sign-off pending. * `SIGNED`: Certification is signed off. 

## Enum

* `NOT_STARTED` (value: `'NOT_STARTED'`)

* `IN_PROGRESS` (value: `'IN_PROGRESS'`)

* `DECISIONS_COMPLETE` (value: `'DECISIONS_COMPLETE'`)

* `SIGNED` (value: `'SIGNED'`)
